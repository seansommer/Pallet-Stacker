↻ ▰LABEL ↓ ↺
  1. Tap to turn. Each tap rotates a case 90° clockwise. The numbered buttons turn the nearest case in that lane. On a keyboard, use 1–5; hold Shift to turn the other way.
  2. Match the case footprint. The guide just ahead of each case has the same shape as the case. Its white tab shows where the sticker should face. Green means both match. Amber means it fits but the label faces inward: it stacks, with a 60-point deduction. Purple means it will fall off.
  3. Find your moment. Make your final correct turn inside the purple timing zone for +100 Perfect points. Early alignment is safe too. Cases lock once they leave the belt.
  4. Stack five layers. Each layer has two rows. Watch a cardboard slip sheet slide onto each completed layer before the next one begins. Finish a pallet to unlock the next: three, four, then five lanes. Faster pallet finishes earn a bonus; clean streaks build up to ×4 points.
  5. Keep going. Misaligned cases don’t count toward the pallet. The line sends a replacement. Eight dropped cases end an arcade run; Practice has unlimited chances.
